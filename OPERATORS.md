# The Material Read

Every argument in this graph is stated in its general form. Real arguments do
not arrive in general form. They arrive attached to a subject: a person, a
group, an institution, a place, a moment. Before any node is applied, read the
subject. The same move can be true, partial, or inverted depending on who it is
about and where they stand, and a reader who skips the read will recite the
right argument at the wrong target.

This file defines the read. It is a protocol, not a node: it has no position of
its own and no objection table. It sits above every node and conditions how all
of them fire.

## The read

Ask: who is the subject, and where do they stand relative to the thing being
argued? Then choose one of four outcomes.

1. **Confirm.** The default applies. Most subjects, most of the time.
2. **Re-register.** The argument holds but the delivery changes. A frightened
   person and a comfortable ideologue can say the same words and deserve
   different answers.
3. **Re-route.** The surface concept is not the governing concept. A different
   node owns the case.
4. **Re-sign.** The conditions invert the conclusion. The rare and serious
   outcome; when it fires, say so openly.

## The five operators

The read runs on five questions. Marked subjects, the ones that trip an
override, are open-ended and cannot be enumerated. The questions that detect
them are few, and these five have covered every case found so far.

**1. Class position.** Where does the subject sit in the arrangement being
argued about? The test case: "police officers need a strong union." Every
pro-union argument in this graph is true, and none of it governs here, because
the subject is not labor facing capital; it is the enforcement arm of the
arrangement unions exist to fight. The word "union" is a decoy. The case
re-routes.

**2. Coercion versus voluntary.** Is the arrangement chosen, or chosen the way
a person chooses to hand over a wallet? A market at gunpoint is not a market.
A yes with a cliff behind it was made by the cliff.

**3. Power asymmetry and scale.** The same formal relation carries different
weight at different sizes. One spare room rented out and a rental empire are
the same transaction on paper and are not the same fact in the world. Scale
does not change the mechanism; it changes what the mechanism does to people.

**4. Who benefits.** Follow the outcome to its beneficiary before crediting
the stated purpose. A blockade of an abortion clinic borrows the sit-in's
entire toolkit; the tactic is ours, the project is domination, and no tactic is
honored for its own sake. The question also runs backward at this framework:
an argument that conveniently exempts our own side has not yet been read.

**5. Historical stage and place.** When and where changes what a thing is. The
flag of an occupied people and the flag of the empire occupying them are both
nationalism, and they are not the same thing. Advice that fits a liberal
democracy can get someone killed under a dictatorship. Arguments calibrated to
one time and place are restated, not recited, anywhere else.

## The sparse principle

Defaults hold. The read is not a license to relativize every claim into fog;
for most subjects the general form of the node is simply correct. Overrides are
rare, marked, and specific, and the burden of showing that a subject is marked
is on the reader making the override.

The five operators above are claimed as the complete override vocabulary. This
is a falsifiable claim. A genuine sixth operator would be a recurring class of
cases where a node's conclusion should shift and none of the five questions
detects why. Anyone who finds one has found a defect in this file, and the file
should then be amended.

## Discipline

The read re-routes arguments. It never excuses abandoning them. "It depends on
the conditions" is the beginning of an answer, and whoever says it owes the
rest: which condition, which operator, and where the case actually routes.

The read applies symmetrically. Claims made by this framework are subject to
the same five questions as claims made against it, and an application of the
read that only ever favors one side is not a read; it is a costume.
