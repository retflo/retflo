retflo v1.1.0 — the honest-strength release.

## What changed

This release came out of adversarial testing: a long-form debate between two frontier models, one defending the framework, one prosecuting it. The defender lost ground it didn't have to lose — and won ground it shouldn't have held. Both findings are now in the framework.

### New argument nodes (7)

- **`auth/finality-kernel`** — the strongest argument in the critic's arsenal ("someone decides last"), stated at full strength and engaged through rather than routed around. The kernel is conceded as fact; everything that matters — who holds it, how big it is, what shape it takes — is contested from there.
- **`rhet/collapse-chain`** — frame-capture defense: detecting narrowing chains that assemble honest concessions into surrender, and the category-expansion counter.
- **`phil/reverse-dominance`** — Boehm's anti-dominance coalition: equality as the species' oldest politics, actively enforced, with the scale constraints stated honestly.
- **`hist/articles-confederation`** — the peacetime self-centralization argument, engaged on the merits.
- **`tech/coordination-costs`** — Coase cuts both ways: falling coordination costs move the optimal boundary away from hierarchy.
- **`econ/rules-vs-discretion`** — the means-end trap disarmed: structural rules vs. discretionary management.
- **`rhet/dignity-asymmetry`** — anti-domination affect routed to the wrong address, and the discipline that keeps the status machinery honest.

### Doctrine

- **Concede facts, refuse frames** replaces "never concede structural ground" throughout (STYLE-GUIDE §4.4). Every Concession cell in every node is now typed `**Fact**` or `**Frame**`.
- **Audience Dynamics & The Dignity Protocol** (STYLE-GUIDE §5) — contempt flows up, dignity flows down; never close the honorable off-ramp; shame attaches to positions, never to questions.
- **CLOSE-CONDITIONS.md** — argument endpoint procedures, now a repo document: eleven named conditions with detection and designated closes.

### Scholarship

- Graeber & Wengrow now cited for what *The Dawn of Everything* actually argues (no single political default) rather than a cooperative-default flattening.
- "Evolutionary biology confirms" tightened to the defensible claim (context-dependent dominant strategy).
- The Convenience Test is now deployed symmetrically, as a genetic-fallacy detector that takes all human-nature arguments off the table.
