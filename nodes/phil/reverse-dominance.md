# Reverse Dominance: Equality Is Enforced, Not Innate

## Position

The critic's picture: hierarchy is the human default, and egalitarian arrangements are fragile departures from it. The anthropological record shows the opposite mechanism — and the mechanism matters more than the averages.

### Boehm's Finding

Christopher Boehm's cross-cultural survey of forager societies (*Hierarchy in the Forest*) documents what he named the **reverse dominance hierarchy**: mobile forager bands maintained political equality for tens of millennia not because nobody sought dominance, but because everyone else actively suppressed whoever did. Ridicule for the boastful hunter. Ostracism for the self-aggrandizer. Exile, and in persistent cases execution, for the would-be dominator. The whole coalition dominates any individual who attempts to dominate — dominance inverted, pointed downward at upstarts.

Three consequences for the human-nature debate:

1. **Egalitarianism is not the absence of politics. It is the oldest politics.** The forager band is not a power vacuum; it is a permanently active anti-dominance coalition. Equality was an achievement, continuously produced, for longer than any other political form has existed.
2. **The desire to dominate and the will to resist domination are both human inheritances.** The critic cites the first and deletes the second. Both are in the record; the question is which one institutions amplify. → `phil/nature.md`
3. **The coalition is collective, undelegated finality.** When the band exiles the upstart, that decision binds him without his consent and without any sovereign delegate. The last word exists — held by everyone, delegated to no one. This is the answer to "who decides last" that predates the question. → `auth/finality-kernel.md`

### What Broke It — Stated Honestly

Forager equality died almost everywhere at the same archaeological moment: storable surplus. Grain doesn't rot; hoards buy guards, dependents, and time; and accumulated advantage compounds faster than informal norms can respond. The defector doesn't have to beat the coalition in a fight — he has to outgrow its reaction speed. Add scale: the coalition's mechanism runs on faces. At band scale, monitoring is free and reputation is total. Among millions of strangers, defection is anonymous and the watching becomes a specialized job — and the specialized watcher is accumulating exactly the asymmetric capacity the norm exists to prevent.

These are the genuine engineering constraints, and the framework states them rather than hiding them, because the design program is built against them: compounding must be interrupted structurally (→ `econ/inequality-ratchet.md`), monitoring must be distributed and automated rather than specialized (→ `auth/inertia-vs-capture.md`), and the norm must be reproduced institutionally rather than assumed (→ `soc/cultural-reproduction.md`).

### Norms and Structures Are Substitutes at the Margin

The stronger a population's anti-dominance practice, the smaller the formal enforcement kernel needed to keep concentration suppressed. High-trust societies already govern with a lighter touch than low-trust ones; the relationship is observable and continuous. This reprices the entire cultural project: building cooperative practice is not utopian preamble — it is kernel-shrinking, the work that directly reduces how much crystallized authority a society requires. Kropotkin's actual value is here. Not a claim that decay stops, but that actively fighting decay — perpetually, structurally, as a constitutive activity — is the thing the species has always done at its best. The fight doesn't end. That isn't the position's weakness; it is its realism.

### The Vigilance Must Welcome the Stress-Tester

A coalition defined by perpetual vigilance against decay needs, more than any other institution, the people who ask "what if one of us defects?" out loud, on purpose. Treating the question as a confession — "whoever asks about the defector intends to be the defector" — converts vigilance into loyalty screening and makes the framework unfalsifiable, which is the failure mode of every guarded orthodoxy the framework critiques. Security engineers who ask "what if someone steals the key" are the reason the lock works. The threat model is the immune system, not the infection.
